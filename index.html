Instrument Time Breakdown

Simulated instrument is split by whether the row had ASEL/AMEL time (airplane) or not (AATD/simulator device). Total airplane time always comes from ASEL + AMEL, never from Total Duration, so simulator sessions never inflate airplane totals.

By Aircraft Make/Model

Grouped by exactly what you type in "Aircraft Make/Model" (case/spacing-insensitive — C172SP, C172K, C172I, and C172N are kept separate). Carried-forward totals are not split by model, since prior logbooks weren't tracked at this level of detail — figures below reflect entries logged in this app.
